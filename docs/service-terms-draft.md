Compatibility and supported versions must be confirmed before launch.

## Accounts and permissions
Protect account access and AI credentials. Only connect devices and access data you are authorized to control. Users select permissions and may revoke them. AUTO mode can perform permitted actions without individual confirmation; users must review the scope and consequences before enabling it. AI outputs may be incorrect. No unauthorized access, surveillance, harmful or unlawful use, or interference with service security.

Describe supported functionality and limitations accurately. Set proportionate suspension grounds, notice and appeal procedures, data access after closure, and service discontinuation arrangements. Avoid blanket liability exclusions; preserve statutory rights and liabilities that cannot lawfully be excluded. Review separate consumer and business terms where appropriate.

Provide the approved privacy notice, verified contact channels and complaint process. Confirm applicable law and jurisdiction without depriving consumers of mandatory local protections.
